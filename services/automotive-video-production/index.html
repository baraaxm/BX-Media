High-End Video & Content Production

Automotive Video Production in Riyadh

BX Media Automotive is a Riyadh-based production company that specialises in video for the automotive industry: vehicle launch reels, brand films, long-form YouTube episodes, event coverage and social cutdowns for car brands, dealerships, detailing studios and motorsport organisers across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

What's included

Vehicle launch films and reels

Hero films and vertical reels for new model reveals, built to run across Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube.

Brand and campaign films

Story-led films for distributors, dealer groups and automotive service brands.

Long-form YouTube production

Multi-episode series and event coverage, such as our nine-episode SAMF motorsport series with ArabGT.

Product and service showcases

Detailing, paint protection and service-centre reels that show the work up close.

Event and motorsport coverage

On-location crews for championships, rallies, track days and launch events.

Social cutdowns

Shoots are repurposed into platform-specific edits so one production feeds more of your channels.

How it works

  1. Brief

    Share your model, audience, launch date and channels. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. Concept and storyboard

    We plan shots, locations, talent and deliverables around your goal.

  3. Production

    Cinematography built around the vehicle: motion, detail and sound.

  4. Post-production

    Edit, colour, sound design and platform-ready cutdowns.

Who it's for

Car Brands, Distributors & Dealerships

Launch campaigns, model reels and creator content for OEMs, distributors and dealer groups.

Detailing, Service Centres & Aftermarket

Showcase reels for detailing studios, paint protection, service centres and parts brands.

Motorsport, Events & Experiences

Championship coverage, YouTube series and experience films for organisers and media partners.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of automotive videos does BX Media produce?

Launch reels, brand films, long-form YouTube episodes, influencer videos, event and motorsport coverage, and detailing or service showcases. We specialise in the automotive industry.

Where does BX Media film?

BX Media is based in Riyadh and films across Saudi Arabia, including past shoots in AlUla, Umluj, Jeddah, Taif, Al Baha, Al-Ahsa, Qassim and Hail. We serve clients across the GCC.
